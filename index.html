        How the plan is chosen
        1. Each load is taken out of its usual hours in the forecast.
        2. Every allowed start time is tried, one load at a time, until no move improves the day.
        3. Plans are ranked by fewest hours at or above 200 kW, then least energy above that line, then less grid backfill while the battery is at reserve, then lower energy cost. A plan never adds overload hours.
